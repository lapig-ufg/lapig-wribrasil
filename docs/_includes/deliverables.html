Products & Deliverables

Products 1 & 2 Deliverables & Technical Assets

Open data, processing routines, technical reports, and interactive spatial analytics from Product 1 (Detailed Sampling Plan, August 17, 2026) and Product 2 (Image-Interpreted Points & Field Sampling Strategy, November 3, 2026).

Field Reference Base (701 Polygons)
701 in situ polygons (~1 ha; 608 classified into 7 categories + 93 unlabeled) with extracted embeddings and S2 series
Pasture Mapping Training Set (36,520 Points/Year)
Visually interpreted pasture / non-pasture points from MapBiomas Col. 4 S2 and 85k Landsat, 2020–2025 (219,120 records, 176 IBGE sheets), as a shapefile with the Google Earth Engine classification script
Pasture Condition Sample Series (63,284 Locations)
50k persistent pasture points + MapBiomas pasture points, 2019–2025 (428,866 records), with Sentinel-2 series and 64-D AlphaEarth embeddings
Similarity & SOM Decision Tables
Top-1 similarity, md3, SOM neuron, dominant class, purity, posteriors, and Clean / Analyze / Remove decisions per record (90×90 final and 35×35 comparison, Parquet)
CBERS-4A Inspection Sample (971 Points)
Stratified sample across 7 categories × Clean / Analyze, with design weights and empty columns for the blind interpretation (CSV)
2025 Pasture Vigor Maps (3 Versions)
Vigor maps on the 2025a, 2025b, and persistent pasture base maps, difference analysis against MapBiomas and GPW, and a 940-point stratified validation sample
Field Revisit Sample & Routes (100 Points)
Goiás 2022 points stratified by degradation score × 2025 vigor, with preliminary routes for the November 2026 campaigns
Interactive Web Map & Inspector
GPU-accelerated Canvas viewer, multi-filters (50k / 12k, years, CVP vigor, md3 slider), spatial bounding box, and charts
Technical Reports (Products 1 & 2)
Product 1: Detailed Sampling Plan and Sampling Improvement Strategies. Product 2: Image-Interpreted Points & Field Sampling Strategy — SOM filtering, CBERS-4A sample, training set, 2025 vigor maps, and field revisit
