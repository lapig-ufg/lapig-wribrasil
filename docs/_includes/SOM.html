Neural Networks & Time Series Curation

SOM Analysis (Self-Organizing Maps)

Methodology based on 12×12 Kohonen Self-Organizing Maps (144 neurons) for organizing multivariate Sentinel-2 time series, evaluating spectral-temporal neighborhood coherence, and filtering candidate pseudo-labels via Bayesian smoothing.

12×12 SOM Topological Curation (144 Neurons)

Component 06 — Under Development

• Input Dimensionality: Standardized monthly indices (NDVI, EVI, NDII, NDWI, PSRI, CAI) structured as 12×J for annual cycles or 72×J for the full 6-year multi-year series (2020–2025).
• Bayesian Smoothing: Dirichlet-Multinomial smoothing over neuron neighborhood compositions (with Normal-Normal conjugate models evaluated as an alternative) to triage candidates into: Keep, Forward for CBERS-4A Inspection, or Reject.
• Preparation & Quality Controls: Feature z-score normalization, missing observation handling, monitoring of quantization and topographic errors, seed stability testing, and strict segregation of reference evidence from candidate pseudo-label frequencies.
