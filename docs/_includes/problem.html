Database Architecture & Repository Structure

Three Complementary Datasets Powering the Curation Pipeline

The repository organizes the pasture mapping benchmark into three linked spatial databases spanning 2019–2025, allowing independent testing of historical reference baselines, persistent vigor-stratified samples, and multi-collection map agreement scenarios.

1. Field Reference Base · Embrapa Cenargen
701 In Situ Polygons (608 Classified + 93 Unlabeled)

Approximately 1 ha field-surveyed polygons distributed across the Cerrado, recording continuous ground-cover fractions (% forage grass, % woody canopy, % ruderal weeds, % bare soil) and altitude. The 608 labeled polygons anchor the supervised similarity ranking across 7 condition classes, while 93 unlabeled polygons are retained for exploratory and unsupervised evaluation.

2. Reference & Validation Candidate Series · 12k Base (2019–2025)
12,009 Inspected & Independent Validation Points

Combines 5,453 visually inspected MapBiomas Collection 4 Sentinel-2 training points and 6,556 MapBiomas 85k Landsat independent validation points (totalling up to 12,483 points in 2019 and 12,009 in 2025). Evaluated against all 701 field areas (~8.4M dot-product pairs/year), achieving a mean cosine similarity of 0.8217 and 67.16% Top-3 neighbor agreement (md3 ≥ 2).

3. Persistent Vigor-Stratified Series · 50k Base (2020–2025)
50,000 Persistent Pasture Points & Col. 11 Scenarios

Sampled from pixels persistently mapped as pasture across all 6 years (2020–2025) in Collection 4 Sentinel-2 and stratified by 2025 vegetative vigor (~35% low, ~44% medium, ~21% high) to counteract the 62.2% productive bias of field samples. Cross-tabulation with Collection 11 separates 44,943 concordant points (89.886%) from 5,057 discordant points (10.114%), defining the Inclusive (62,009) vs. Agreement (56,952) sensitivity scenarios across 35.05M pairs/year (mean similarity 0.8245; 67.52% with md3 ≥ 2).
