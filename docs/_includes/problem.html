Database Architecture & Repository Structure

Three Complementary Datasets Powering the Curation Pipeline

The repository organizes the pasture mapping benchmark into three linked spatial databases spanning 2019–2025: the field reference base, the visually interpreted MapBiomas pasture / non-pasture points used to generate the pasture maps, and the pasture sample series used to categorize samples into the 7 condition classes and generate the pasture degradation map.

1. Field Reference Base · Embrapa Cenargen
701 In Situ Polygons (608 Classified + 93 Unlabeled)

Approximately 1 ha field-surveyed polygons distributed across the Cerrado, recording continuous ground-cover fractions (% forage grass, % woody canopy, % ruderal weeds, % bare soil) and altitude. The 608 labeled polygons anchor the Top-1 category transfer across 7 condition classes and characterize the SOM neurons, while 93 unlabeled polygons are retained for exploratory and unsupervised evaluation.

2. Pasture Mapping Training Set · MapBiomas Visual Interpretation (2020–2025)
36,520 Pasture / Non-Pasture Points per Year

MapBiomas Collection 4 Sentinel-2 training points: 15,669 points per year — 5,839 to 6,222 pasture and 9,447 to 9,830 non-pasture (94,014 records). MapBiomas 85k Landsat validation points: 20,851 points per year — 6,528 to 6,570 pasture and 14,281 to 14,323 non-pasture (125,106 records). Together, 36,520 points per year (219,120 records, 176 IBGE map sheets), all visually interpreted, with no map-derived samples: the set used to generate the pasture maps.

3. Pasture Condition Sample Series · 50k Base + MapBiomas Pasture Points (2019–2025)
63,284 Pasture Locations · 428,866 Records

The 50,000 persistent pasture points (350,000 records; Collection 4 Sentinel-2 pasture in every year 2020–2025, stratified by 2025 vigor: ~35% low, ~44% medium, ~21% high) plus only the pasture points of dataset 2 (6,288 Sentinel-2 and 6,996 Landsat points; 78,866 records). Used to categorize the samples into the 7 condition classes — Top-1 embedding similarity to the Embrapa references and SOM filtering: 219,810 records kept (Clean), 65,369 flagged for CBERS-4A inspection (Analyze), 143,687 removed (Remove) — and to generate the pasture degradation map.
