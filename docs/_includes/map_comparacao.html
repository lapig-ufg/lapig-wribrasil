Stratum code = a b MB (1 = pasture). All difference strata fall between 000 (agreement, non-pasture) and 111 (agreement, pasture): they are transition areas.

Attribute Distributions by Stratum

  • Category 6 (MB pasture, a and b not): in the north (PA/TO), pasture with trees and regeneration; MapBiomas and GPW call it pasture, a and b do not.
  • Category 5 (a and b pasture, MB not): includes native grassland on rugged terrain, partly flagged as natural grassland by GPW, and plots where MapBiomas maps another use.
  • Categories 1–4 (only a or only b differs): edges and isolated pixels of the same patches; a and b probabilities nearly equal and close to the threshold.

Validation Sample for the Difference Regions

Stratified random sampling over the 8 strata of the a × b × MapBiomas cross-tabulation, with equal probability within each stratum. Difference strata receive far more points than their area. A single sample estimates the accuracy of 2025a, 2025b, MapBiomas and GPW (Stehman, 2014).

Method & Caveats

  • Common 10 m grid (EPSG:4326): a, b and MapBiomas aligned pixel by pixel; GPW (30 m) read by nearest neighbor.
  • Clipped to the IBGE biome boundary; areas are the sum of true pixel areas per 0.25° cell, computed locally in Earth Engine.
  • GPW: only v1 (up to 2022) is accessible; part of the disagreement is real land-use change between 2022 and 2025.
  • Figure labels in the Sentinel-2 windows and boxplots are in Portuguese (seca = dry, chuva = rainy, diferenças = differences).
