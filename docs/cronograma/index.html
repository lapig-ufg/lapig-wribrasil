Granular Technical Tracking

Detailed Status of the 18 Methodological Components

Breakdown of the eight technical components established in the Product 1 Report (Detailed Sampling Plan and Sampling Improvement Strategies — August 17, 2026) and updated in the Product 2 Report (November 3, 2026), tracking data assembly, metric extraction, dual filtering, visual inspection, and field validation, plus the pasture base map training set and 2025 vigor maps added in Product 2 (components 09–10), followed by the next methodological steps toward the 2020–2025 maps (components 11–14) and the property-level analyses of Product 4 (components 15–18).

Component 01 · Status: Assembled
1. Field Reference Base (Embrapa Cenargen)
701 polygons of approximately 1 ha surveyed in the field across the Cerrado biome; 608 classified across 7 pasture condition categories and 93 retained without a consolidated class for unsupervised analysis.
Component 02 · Status: Extracted
2. Sentinel-2 Spectral-Temporal Series
Monthly composites (2020–2025) extracted for all reference polygons and candidate points using ~60-day moving window joint medians (NDVI, NDWI,CAI/SWIR ratios); audit of valid observations in progress.
Component 03 · Status: Extracted
3. Annual Latent Embeddings (AlphaEarth Foundations)
64-dimensional AlphaEarth Foundations vectors extracted per year (2020–2025); verification and L2 norm renormalization of mean reference vectors applied prior to cosine similarity calculation.
Component 04 · Status: Assembled
4. Candidate Points Universe (62,009 Points)
62,009 candidate samples assembled with full provenance preserved (50,000 persistent vigor-stratified Sentinel-2 points + 5,453 Col. 4 S2 points + 6,556 Landsat 85k validation points), structured into Inclusive (62,009) and Collection 11 Agreement (56,952) sensitivity scenarios.
Component 05 · Status: Applied
5. Embedding-Based Similarity Filtering
Top-1 similarity against the 608 classified Embrapa Cenargen field references and the md3 agreement indicator computed for all 428,866 candidate point-year records (63,284 locations, 2019–2025); the transferred preliminary category feeds the SOM stage, and the 4,079 records (1.0%) without a transferred category were removed.
Component 06 · Status: Applied
6. SOM-Based Filtering (Self-Organizing Maps)
Final configuration: a single 90×90 grid (8,100 neurons) trained on all years, with Dirichlet-Multinomial smoothing (a 35×35 grid and Normal-Normal smoothing were tested for comparison). Of the 428,866 candidate records, 219,810 were kept (CLEAN, 51.3%), 65,369 were flagged for CBERS-4A inspection (ANALYZE, 15.2%), and 143,687 were removed (REMOVE, 33.5%); 92.3% of the 2020–2025 CLEAN records are productive pasture.
Component 07 · Status: Sample Selected
7. CBERS-4A Visual Inspection (~2 m Resolution)
Stratified sample of 971 locations (one year each, 2020–2025) drawn from the CLEAN and ANALYZE records across 14 strata (7 categories × 2 SOM decisions): up to 100 CLEAN and 50 ANALYZE per category, with all available CLEAN locations taken for the two rarest classes. Interpretation on pan-sharpened 2 m CBERS-4A imagery with a blind protocol has not yet started.
Component 08 · Status: Revisit Scheduled
8. Field Data Collection & Map Validation
100 points from the 2022 Goiás field survey selected for revisit by stratified random sampling over nine degradation × 2025 vigor strata (11 or 12 points each). Campaigns scheduled for November 9–16 and November 23–27, 2026, to check whether each disagreement comes from change since 2022 or from error in the vigor map.
Component 09 · Status: Complete
9. Pasture Base Map Training Set
New pasture / non-pasture training set for 2020–2025 using only visually interpreted samples, with no map-derived samples or map-based filters: 36,520 points per year (about 12,500 pasture and 24,000 non-pasture), 219,120 point-year records in total, linked to the 176 IBGE map sheets of the Cerrado (maximum 500 samples per sheet).
Component 10 · Status: Generated (3 Versions)
10. 2025 Pasture Vigor Maps
Sentinel-2 vigor maps for 2025 generated for three candidate pasture base maps: 2025a (new training set), 2025b (2025a plus geographic coordinates), and persistent MapBiomas pasture (2020–2025). Versions 2025a and 2025b map 52.9 and 52.8 Mha of pasture, differ on 1.0% of the Cerrado, and agree with MapBiomas Collection 4 on about 92%.
Component 11 · Status: Next Step
11. Pasture Base Map Choice (2025 Validation Sample)
Interpretation of a 940-point stratified random sample over the eight strata of the 2025a × 2025b × MapBiomas crossing, estimating the accuracy of 2025a, 2025b, MapBiomas, and Global Pasture Watch with a single sample (Stehman, 2014) to choose the pasture base map for the full series.
Component 12 · Status: Next Step
12. Training Set Consolidation
Corroboration rates from the CBERS-4A inspection, by category and SOM decision, will decide whether ANALYZE records are recovered for training and whether the rare categories (Pasto com Lenhosas, Miscelânea) are merged before the Random Forest training.
Component 13 · Status: Next Step
13. Annual 10 m Pasture & Vigor Maps (2020–2025)
Extension of pasture and vigor mapping to the full 2020–2025 series with the chosen base map, using the visually interpreted training set (36,520 points per year, 176 IBGE map sheets); the five border sheets without samples will require neighbor-sheet models or added samples (Product 3, December 1, 2026).
Component 14 · Status: Next Step
14. Pasture Condition Classification (Random Forest)
Random Forest training for the pasture condition categories with the approved samples, using spatial blocks to minimize autocorrelation leakage between training and evaluation, followed by independent validation with image and field data.
Component 15 · Status: Initiated (Product 4)
15. Land-Tenure & Environmental Database
Assembly of the LAPIG land-tenure and environmental database (SIGEF + SICAR + FBDS permanent preservation areas) to integrate the 10 m pasture maps with rural property boundaries.
Component 16 · Status: Planned (Product 4)
16. Property-Level Land-Use Trajectories
Cross-tabulation of the pasture maps with MapBiomas (1985–2025) and PRODES to reconstruct native vegetation → pasture → cropland trajectories, with environmental liabilities and assets aggregated by rural property, municipality, state, biome, and watershed.
Component 17 · Status: Planned (Product 4)
17. DCF Indicators: Soy Conversion Risk
Property-level soy conversion potential from land use, native vegetation cover, pasture degradation, slope/aptitude, and property boundaries (2024–2030 scenarios), together with the Forest Code compliance balance (Legal Reserve and APP deficits/surpluses).
Component 18 · Status: Planned (Product 4)
18. Pasture Restoration Potential
Property-level pasture restoration potential from GPP trends, vegetative vigor, bare soil exposure, and historical land-use persistence.
