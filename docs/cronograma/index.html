Granular Technical Tracking

Detailed Status of the 8 Methodological Components

Breakdown of the eight technical components established in the Product 1 Report (Detailed Sampling Plan and Sampling Improvement Strategies — August 17, 2026), tracking data assembly, metric extraction, dual filtering, visual inspection, and field validation.

Component 01 · Status: Assembled
1. Field Reference Base (Embrapa Cenargen)
701 polygons of approximately 1 ha surveyed in the field across the Cerrado biome; 608 classified across 7 pasture condition categories and 93 retained without a consolidated class for unsupervised analysis.
Component 02 · Status: Extracted
2. Sentinel-2 Spectral-Temporal Series
Monthly composites (2020–2025) extracted for all reference polygons and candidate points using ~60-day moving window joint medians (NDVI, NDWI,CAI/SWIR ratios); audit of valid observations in progress.
Component 03 · Status: Extracted
3. Annual Latent Embeddings (AlphaEarth Foundations)
64-dimensional AlphaEarth Foundations vectors extracted per year (2020–2025); verification and L2 norm renormalization of mean reference vectors applied prior to cosine similarity calculation.
Component 04 · Status: Assembled
4. Candidate Points Universe (62,009 Points)
62,009 candidate samples assembled with full provenance preserved (50,000 persistent vigor-stratified Sentinel-2 points + 5,453 Col. 4 S2 points + 6,556 Landsat 85k validation points), structured into Inclusive (62,009) and Collection 11 Agreement (56,952) sensitivity scenarios.
Component 05 · Status: Preliminary (Active Milestone)
5. Embedding-Based Similarity Filtering
Top-1/Top-3 cosine similarity matching against the 608 classified field polygons (~35.05M pairs/year for the 50k base and ~8.4M pairs/year for the 12k base), modal neighbor concordance metric (md3), separation margins, and sensitivity thresholds evaluated across Scenarios A through E.
Component 06 · Status: Under Development
6. SOM-Based Filtering (Self-Organizing Maps)
12×12 Kohonen neural grid (144 neurons) clustering annual Sentinel-2 phenological signatures; multivariate dimensionality, normalization, neighborhood topology, and Dirichlet-Multinomial Bayesian smoothing under evaluation to classify neurons into Keep, Inspect, or Remove.
Component 07 · Status: Planning
7. CBERS-4A Visual Inspection (~2 m Resolution)
Stratified random sampling (~1,040 points for a 3% global margin of error at 95% confidence) of borderline candidates after embedding-SOM selection, executed on pan-sharpened 2 m CBERS-4A imagery with a blind multi-interpreter protocol.
Component 08 · Status: Planning
8. Field Data Collection & Map Validation
Targeted field campaign across approximately 100 localities in the Cerrado biome (with logistical priority in Goiás and stratified multi-interpreter imagery expansion) for independent validation of the 10 m Random Forest pasture map.
